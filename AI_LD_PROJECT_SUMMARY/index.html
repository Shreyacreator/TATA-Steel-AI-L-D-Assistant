Executive Summary

This internship project focuses on solving the challenge of personalized learning and real-time training support in a large industrial workforce environment. The proposed AI solution leverages Generative AI, Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), employee skill profiling and enterprise knowledge retrieval to improve learning outcomes.

Week 1 – Business Problem Discovery

Objective

Understand Learning & Development challenges in large industrial organizations.

Key Challenges Identified

  • Low training effectiveness
  • Lack of personalized learning
  • Knowledge loss from experienced workforce
  • Difficulty finding SOP information quickly
  • Skill gap visibility issues

Primary Problem Statement

Lack of Personalized Learning and Real-Time Training Support Across a Diverse Workforce.

Business Impact

Problem Impact
Knowledge Loss Longer onboarding time
Generic Training Low engagement
Skill Gaps Reduced productivity

Week 4 – RAG & Personalization

Retrieval-Augmented Generation was implemented to improve answer quality and accuracy. The AI system retrieves relevant SOP content from PDF documents and combines it with Gemini responses.

PDF Documents ↓ Text Extraction ↓ Embeddings ↓ FAISS Vector Database ↓ Relevant Context ↓ Gemini AI ↓ Final Response
