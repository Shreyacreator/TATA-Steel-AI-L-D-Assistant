🧭 Week 2: AI Use Case Design

User Persona

Shopfloor Operator requiring instant operational guidance.

User Journey

  1. Select employee profile.
  2. Ask a training or SOP question.
  3. AI retrieves relevant document content.
  4. Gemini generates a personalized response.
  5. Training recommendation is displayed.

AI Roles

  • Knowledge Assistant
  • AI Coach
  • Skill Recommender
  • Quiz Generator

Expected Impact

Faster learning and reduced time spent searching manuals.
