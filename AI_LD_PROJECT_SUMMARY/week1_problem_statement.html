📘 Week 1: Business Problem First (Not AI First)

Theme

Think like a consultant, not a coder.

Primary Problem

Lack of personalized learning and real-time training support across a diverse workforce.

Secondary Opportunity

Capture and preserve knowledge from experienced employees.

Key Challenges

  • Skill gap visibility
  • Low training effectiveness
  • Lack of personalization
  • Knowledge loss

Business Impact

  • Longer onboarding time
  • Inconsistent skill levels
  • Increased dependency on experts
  • Lower productivity

Why AI?

AI can provide role-based recommendations and instant SOP-based answers.
