Hot Metal Handling PPE:
- Helmet
- Face shield
- Heat resistant gloves
- Fire-retardant clothing
- Safety shoes

Lockout-Tagout (LOTO):
1. Shut down equipment.
2. Isolate all energy sources.
3. Apply locks and tags.
4. Verify zero energy.
5. Perform work.
6. Remove lockout and restore service.

Crane Safety:
- Inspect hooks and slings before use.
- Never exceed rated load.
- Maintain communication with signalers.
